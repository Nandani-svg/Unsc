How marks are worked out

Claude reads the chit and grades five criteria out of 10, and estimates how likely the wording is AI-written. A stock-phrase scan can raise that estimate but never lower it. The page combines them with fixed weights, scales into the official range and applies the AI rule. Claude never sets the final number directly.

CriterionNormalSubst.
Relevance to agenda30%25%
Substance / depth20%30%
Factual accuracy15%20%
Chit format25%15%
Diplomatic language10%10%

AI rule: AI likelihood 75% or more gets the minimum mark (0.1 / 1). 50–74% is capped at the average (0.5 / 3). Below 50% no penalty.
Other gates: no "From" line ×0.7; off-topic (relevance ≤ 2) capped near the minimum. Normal marks round to 0.1, substantive to 0.5.
